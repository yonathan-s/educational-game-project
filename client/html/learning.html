The Tudor Dynasty

Before you can escape the court, you must understand it. The Tudors ruled England for over a century, and their story is one of war, marriage, and religion that reshaped the entire kingdom.

It began in 1485, when Henry Tudor defeated Richard III at the Battle of Bosworth, ending decades of civil war between the houses of Lancaster and York. His son, Henry VIII, would go on to become the most infamous monarch in English history — six marriages, a break from the Catholic Church, and a court thick with ambition and suspicion.

Hampton Court Palace, where your escape begins, was not even built for a king. It was seized from Cardinal Wolsey, once Henry's most powerful advisor, after he fell from royal favour. Nothing at this court stays certain for long.
