Life at Court

Now you know the dynasty. Next, you need to know the danger. Tudor court life looked glamorous from the outside — velvet, feasts, music — but underneath it ran on suspicion.

Courtiers watched one another constantly. A whispered rumour, a letter to the wrong ambassador, or an alliance with the wrong family could end in the Tower of London. Spies moved freely among nobles, servants, and even priests, reporting back anything that smelled of treason or foreign loyalty.

This is why you now stand accused. Someone at court believes your loyalties lie elsewhere. To walk free, you will need to prove — through your knowledge of this kingdom's history — that you belong to it.
